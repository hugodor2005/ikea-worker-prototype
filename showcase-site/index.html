TEK830 · Digital Innovation Prototype

Clock it.
Speak up.

An anonymous feedback feature built into the clock-out systems IKEA's suppliers already use — turning every shift's end into a chance to surface working-condition issues before they become risks.

How it works

From clock-out to action

A simple three-step process that turns the end of a shift into an opportunity for anonymous feedback.

STEP 01

Clock out

The worker finishes their shift and clocks out using the system already available at the workplace.

→
STEP 02

Speak up

The worker gets the opportunity to leave anonymous feedback about their working conditions.

→
STEP 03

Take action

The feedback reaches the relevant manager through the dashboard and can be acted upon.

Walkthrough

See it in action

A short demo of a worker clocking out, leaving feedback, and that feedback landing on the compliance manager's dashboard.
