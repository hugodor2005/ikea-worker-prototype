Clock it.
Speak up.

An anonymous feedback feature built into the clock-out systems IKEA's suppliers already use — turning every shift's end into a chance to surface working-condition issues before they become risks.

The Problem

Traditional supplier audits provide valuable insights, but they may not capture every issue workers experience in their daily working environment.

Why does this matter?

Identifying issues related to working hours, wages, workplace safety and treatment is essential for improving working conditions across IKEA's supply chain.

Our Solution

Clock It integrates a simple feedback feature into the clock-out process at IKEA's suppliers.

How does it work?

Workers can provide anonymous feedback about their working conditions at the end of their shifts.

The collected information is organized in a dashboard, helping IKEA's Sustainability and Compliance Manager identify patterns and potential concerns.

From clock-out to action

A simple three-step process that turns the end of a shift into an opportunity for anonymous feedback.

STEP 01

Clock out

The worker finishes their shift and clocks out using the system already available at the workplace.

→
STEP 02

Speak up

The worker gets the opportunity to leave anonymous feedback about their working conditions.

→
STEP 03

Take action

The feedback reaches the relevant manager through the dashboard and can be acted upon.

See it in action

A short demo of a worker clocking out, leaving feedback, and that feedback landing on the compliance manager's dashboard.
